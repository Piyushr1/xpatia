## Why Location Makes or Breaks a Restaurant

Studies show that location accounts for 50-60% of a restaurant's success. Yet many owners still choose locations based on gut feeling or availability rather than data.

## Key Factors to Analyze

### 1. Demographics
- Population density in 1km, 3km, and 5km radius
- Income levels and spending patterns
- Age distribution of potential customers
- Household composition

### 2. Competition Analysis
- Direct competitors within walking distance
- Complementary businesses nearby
- Market saturation levels
- Competitor performance patterns

### 3. Accessibility
- Foot traffic patterns
- Parking availability
- Public transport connectivity
- Visibility from main roads

### 4. Commercial Viability
- Rental costs vs. potential revenue
- Anchor stores and foot traffic generators
- Area development trends
- Lease terms and flexibility
