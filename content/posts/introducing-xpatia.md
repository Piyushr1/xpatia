## Why Location Intelligence Matters

Every business decision has a geographic component. Whether you're opening a new restaurant, expanding retail presence, or analyzing market opportunities, understanding "where" is as crucial as understanding "what" and "when."

## The Problem We're Solving

Traditional location intelligence platforms are expensive and inaccessible to small businesses and startups. Premium GIS tools can cost thousands of dollars per month, putting data-driven location decisions out of reach for most businesses.

## What is Xpatia?

Xpatia is a location intelligence platform built on open-source data, combining:

- **Points of Interest (POI) data** from OpenStreetMap
- **Population demographics** from WorldPop
- **Custom spatial analytics** tailored to your business needs

Our mission: Make location intelligence affordable and accessible to businesses of all sizes.

## Who We Serve

- Restaurant chains planning expansion
- Retail businesses optimizing store networks  
- Real estate developers assessing opportunities
- Market researchers needing hyperlocal data
