## The Data Challenge

When we started building Xpatia, we faced a choice: use expensive proprietary data sources or leverage open-source alternatives.

We chose open-source. Here's why.

## OpenStreetMap: The Wikipedia of Maps

OpenStreetMap (OSM) provides:
- **100+ million POIs globally** - restaurants, shops, amenities
- **Community-verified accuracy** - constantly updated by local contributors
- **Complete flexibility** - query exactly what you need
- **Zero licensing costs** - truly open data

## WorldPop: Population Data at Scale

WorldPop offers:
- **100m resolution population grids** globally
- **Demographic breakdowns** by age and gender
- **Regular updates** with latest census data
- **Free for commercial use**

## The Open-Source Advantage

### Cost Efficiency
No per-query fees or usage limits. This savings gets passed to our clients.

### Transparency
You can verify our data sources and methodology. No black-box algorithms.

### Customization
We can combine multiple open datasets to create insights specific to your business.

### Community Innovation
Standing on the shoulders of thousands of contributors worldwide.

## Quality Assurance

Open-source doesn't mean unverified. We:
- Cross-reference multiple sources
- Apply data quality filters
- Validate against known ground truth
- Update regularly from upstream sources

## The Result

Affordable location intelligence that rivals premium platforms - at a fraction of the cost.
