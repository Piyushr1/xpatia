# About Xpatia

Xpatia is a location intelligence platform making spatial analytics accessible to businesses of all sizes.

## Our Story

Built by data professionals with 10+ years of analytics and GIS experience, Xpatia was founded on a simple belief: location intelligence shouldn't be a luxury reserved for large enterprises.

## Our Approach

We leverage open-source geospatial data (OpenStreetMap, WorldPop) combined with advanced analytics to deliver insights that traditionally cost 10x more.

## Our Services

**Location Audits** - Analyze your existing locations to understand success patterns

**Site Selection** - Find the optimal location for your next outlet using data

**Market Analysis** - Understand demographics, competition, and opportunities

## Our Mission

Democratize location intelligence - making data-driven location decisions accessible to every business.
